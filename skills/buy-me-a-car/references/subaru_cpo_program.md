# Subaru Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Program

Subaru CPO is one of the strongest OEM CPO programs for used Subaru vehicles. Understanding eligibility and embedded value is critical for negotiations.

## Eligibility Criteria

Per subaru.com/certified:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Model year | Current year + 4 preceding (e.g., 2026 program covers 2022-2026) |
| Mileage | Under 80,000 mi |
| Inspection | Must pass 152-point Subaru-certified inspection |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Subaru retailer |

A 2024 Subaru with 20,000 mi at a Subaru dealer in 2026 is fully eligible.

A 2019 Subaru is NOT eligible (>5 years old). Even if mileage is under 80k, the time limit excludes it.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| Powertrain Warranty | 7 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date |
| Deductible | $0 |
| Roadside Assistance | 24/7 included |
| Transferable | Yes (to subsequent buyers within warranty period) |
| Trip Interruption | $500 reimbursement |
| Rental Car Allowance | $35/day for covered repairs |

The 7yr/100k starts from the original in-service date, NOT the CPO purchase date. So a 2024 Subaru that went into service in 2024 has CPO coverage through 2031 (7 years) or 100,000 mi, whichever comes first.

## Embedded Value

Compare CPO to third-party extended powertrain warranties:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Subaru CPO (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Subaru Added Security (separate purchase post-CPO) | $1,500-2,500 |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance, etc.) | $2,000-2,800 for equivalent coverage |
| Liberty Mutual / Geico mechanical breakdown | $1,800-2,300 |

CPO is functionally a $2,000-2,500 embedded value when the dealer enrolls it as part of the sale.

## What CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, batteries — typical)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear)
- Items "modified" or "altered" by aftermarket parts
- Routine maintenance (oil changes, fluid services — separate program: Subaru Added Security)

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math

For a 2024 Forester Limited 20k mi:

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| Subaru CPO included | $30,000 OTD |
| Equivalent: Non-CPO + third-party 7/100k warranty | $30,000 OTD - $2,000 (no embedded warranty) + $2,000-2,500 (separate warranty buy) = $30,000-30,500 |
| Non-CPO + self-insure | $30,000 OTD, accept warranty risk |

A non-CPO Subaru priced the same as a CPO Subaru is effectively $2,000 more expensive.

## Negotiating CPO

If a Subaru is CPO-eligible but the dealer has not enrolled it (sometimes happens with quick trade-in turnaround):

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Subaru CPO program as part of the sale? This adds the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty and is a standard Subaru-certified retailer offering for sub-80k mi cars.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for ~$500-800 internal cost. They may accept this as part of the deal at no additional charge to you, especially if you've reduced your OTD ask elsewhere.

If the dealer charges separately for CPO enrollment ($1,000-1,500), it's usually still better value than buying third-party post-sale.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 5 Years Old)

A 2019 Subaru is ineligible. Options:

1. **Subaru Added Security (SAS) Maintenance Plan** — separate program (Subaru's extended warranty), available at any age/mileage threshold. Plans like 60-month/65,000-mile coverage.
2. **Third-party extended warranty** — buy elsewhere
3. **Self-insure** — accept warranty risk

Typical NJ Subaru dealer SAS pricing for a 60-month / 65,000-mile plan on an out-of-CPO 2019 Outback Limited (~50k mi at purchase):
- Powertrain only: approximately $2,500 to $2,900
- Comprehensive: approximately $4,000 to $4,500

These are dealer-marked-up. Third-party equivalents (CARCHEX, Endurance, Liberty Mutual mechanical breakdown) may be cheaper. Compare quotes from at least two third-party providers before signing dealer SAS.

For out-of-CPO cars, the embedded value math reverses: a buyer should expect the price to be $2,000 lower than a comparable CPO car (since they lose that embedded warranty).

## CPO Verification

Confirm CPO status before closing:

- Ask for the CPO certificate (PDF)
- Ask for the 152-point inspection report
- Verify warranty start date matches original in-service date
- Confirm transferability documentation

Without these in hand, "CPO" claims are unverified.

## Related Subaru Programs

- **Subaru Added Security (SAS)** — Subaru's separately-sold extended warranty. Different from CPO.
- **Subaru Loyalty Discount** — for return Subaru customers; typically $500-1,000 off MSRP on new
- **Subaru College Graduate** — $500 off
- **Subaru Military Discount** — $500 off
- **Subaru Owner Loyalty Bonus Cash** — varies, region-specific incentive

Most of these apply to NEW Subaru purchases, not used. Used Subaru CPO is its own thing.
