## Before the visit

- Confirm independent inspection access and any booking/cancellation terms.
- Read the full vehicle history, service records, title/lien information and
  applicable warranty or CPO certificate. A clean history report does not prove
  there was no damage.
- Compare fees using current registering-jurisdiction evidence.
- Check insurance, payment instrument, verified payee and document requirements.
- Prepare questions from actual gaps; do not invent competing offers, readiness
  to deposit, financing approval or commitments.

## Inspection and test drive

Use an independent mechanic for diagnosis. Record visible condition, tire age and
wear, leaks, warning lights, maintenance records, accessory operation and road
behavior under normal safe driving. Follow road rules and seller instructions;
do not deliberately hit potholes or perform abrupt emergency maneuvers as a test.

Panel gaps, odors, fluid appearance or post-shutdown fans may warrant investigation
but do not establish a particular defect by themselves. Obtain a written inspection
report and repair estimate before applying a monetary adjustment. A guessed mileage
or repair deduction is not a verified valuation.
