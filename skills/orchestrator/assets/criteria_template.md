|---|---|

| Plate plan | {new or transfer; eligibility and current quoted fees} |

| Payment instrument logistics | {accepted instrument, verified payee, issuer cutoff} |

Keep contact information in the private workflow only when needed for an authorized action. Do not collect account numbers or identity documents merely to complete this table. Any material change creates a new criteria revision and triggers quote revalidation.
