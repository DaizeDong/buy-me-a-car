# Independent inspection booking

Verify the specific vehicle and seller availability before arranging inspection.
Use current shop contacts and actual written quotes; this reference does not
maintain a provider price or phone-number directory.

## Scope and selection

Match the provider to the vehicle and task: lift access, diagnostic scan,
road test, structural/body assessment, tire/brake measurements, leak inspection,
and EV battery or high-voltage expertise where relevant. Confirm whether
specialized tests are included and what the written report will contain.
Ask about independence from the seller and any relevant qualifications.

A mobile service may not offer lift, alignment or full battery diagnostics.
Record those limits instead of treating different inspection packages as equal.

## Booking contract

Before submitting, establish provider, VIN, location, seller permission, date/time,
inspection scope, total price, travel charges, cancellation deadline, refund terms,
required contact details and report timing. Obtain authorization for the actual
booking and any payment. Parallel reservations must be explicitly covered;
do not reserve every shortlisted car by default.

Save the provider receipt under the private cycle directory. If confirmation is
missing, mark the booking uncertain and reconcile before retrying or choosing a
second provider. Cancellation also needs the provider's confirmation and must
respect the buyer's scope. Do not expose contact data through public fixtures.

## After inspection

Retain the original report. Check VIN/date, actual measurements, limitations,
photos and diagnostic findings. Distinguish findings from speculation and
missing access. Ask the provider about unresolved issues and get repair estimates
before assigning a price adjustment.

Present the buyer with the evidence, expected repair/maintenance work, remaining
uncertainty, and options to proceed, counter or walk. Do not call the vehicle
defect-free, promise a future resale value or infer an accident solely from a
cosmetic irregularity.
