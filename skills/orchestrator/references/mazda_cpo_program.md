# Mazda Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Program (as of 2026-05-18)

> **last_verified**: 2026-05-18 (skill stress test iteration 5 + P0-P5 consolidation)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-05-18 timestamp marks last full audit.

Mazda CPO is a single-tier mainstream program with a moderate-coverage 7yr/100k powertrain extension and a standard 12mo/12k limited B2B add. Mazda's reliability is statistically strong (consistently top-5 in Consumer Reports), so Mazda CPO embedded value runs slightly lower than Subaru/Hyundai CPO at the median — but the lower median reflects lower expected claim cost, not lower coverage depth.

The standout brand-specific buyer-side features:
- All Mazda CX-5 / CX-50 / CX-90 / Mazda3 / Mazda6 / MX-5 Miata since 2018 have **standard Apple CarPlay + Android Auto** — useful Phase 1 / Phase 6 anchor for connectivity-focused buyers.
- The **CX-50 is assembled at MTMUS Huntsville AL** (Mazda Toyota Manufacturing USA joint venture with Toyota) — domestic-content positioning relevant to certain state programs and tariff-discussion contexts.
- The **MX-5 Miata** is the only mass-market enthusiast roadster on the CPO market — embedded value for Miata buyers is structurally different (enthusiast resale dynamics dominate the CPO premium math).

## Eligibility Criteria

Per Mazda Certified Pre-Owned at mazdausa.com/certified-pre-owned:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age from original in-service date | **Max 6 years** (e.g., 2026 program covers 2020-2026 in-service vehicles) |
| Mileage | **Under 80,000 mi** |
| Inspection | Must pass Mazda's **150-point certified inspection** |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Mazda dealer |
| Title / history | Clean title; no major accident on CARFAX / AutoCheck; no flood, salvage, fire, or rebuilt branding |

A 2022 Mazda CX-5 Touring AWD with 32,000 mi sold by a Mazda dealer in 2026 is fully eligible (4 yr / 32k mi).

A 2020 Mazda3 Hatchback with 75,000 mi in 2026 is at the eligibility edge: 6 yr from in-service if early-2020 build, under 80k mi — eligible.

A 2019 Mazda in 2026 is generally NOT CPO eligible (>6 years from in-service).

### Single-tier program

Mazda CPO is a single tier — no Standard/Premium split like Honda True Certified. All eligible Mazda units get the same coverage package.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Powertrain warranty extension** | **7 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** |
| **Limited bumper-to-bumper extension** | **12 months / 12,000 miles** added (from CPO purchase date, NOT from original in-service) |
| Deductible | $0 |
| Roadside Assistance | 7 years / 100,000 mi 24/7 included (from in-service date) |
| Transferable | Yes (to subsequent buyers within warranty period) |
| Trip Interruption | $50/day, up to $500 total |
| Rental Car Allowance | $35/day for covered repairs (max 6 days) |
| Sirius XM trial | 3-month on enrollment |

The 7yr/100k powertrain coverage starts from the **original in-service date**, NOT the CPO purchase date.

### Mild-Hybrid / Plug-In Hybrid models (CX-90 PHEV / CX-50 Hybrid 2025+)

Note: Mazda's hybrid lineup is limited as of 2026-05-18.

| Model | Powertrain | CPO coverage |
|---|---|---|
| CX-90 PHEV (2024+) | 2.5L PHEV with 17.8 kWh pack | Standard 7/100k powertrain covers PHEV battery + drive motor (no separate hybrid component tier) |
| CX-50 Hybrid (2025+) | 2.5L Hybrid (Toyota-derived powertrain from MTMUS Huntsville JV) | Standard 7/100k powertrain covers hybrid battery + electric motor |
| MX-30 EV (discontinued 2024, US market only had 2022-2023) | Small 35 kWh BEV pack | Standard 7/100k powertrain + 8/100k EV battery coverage (federal mandate minimum); very few units on used market |

Mazda does NOT currently have a Toyota-TCUV-style separate Hybrid Component 10/150k coverage tier — even though the CX-50 Hybrid shares its powertrain with the RAV4 Hybrid. Mazda rolls hybrid coverage into the standard powertrain line. This is a structural disadvantage vs TCUV for hybrid-focused buyers.

## Embedded Value

Compare Mazda CPO to third-party extended powertrain warranties:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Mazda CPO (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Mazda Total Confidence Plan (separate, post-CPO) | $1,400-$2,400 layered on top |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance, Olive) | $1,800-$2,600 for equivalent 7/100k powertrain |

**Mazda CPO is functionally a $1,000-$1,800 embedded value** when the dealer enrolls it as part of the sale. This is slightly LOWER than Hyundai/Kia CPO ($1,500-$2,500) and Subaru CPO ($2,000-$2,500) because:
1. Mazda's reliability statistics mean third-party plans price Mazda lower than Hyundai/Subaru
2. Mazda's powertrain term (7/100k) matches Honda/Toyota but the brand has fewer premium-coverage selling points (no diesel tier, no EV tier of substance, no hybrid-specific tier)

**Market premium typical**: **$600-$1,200 over identical non-CPO same VIN** at typical US Mazda dealers. CX-5 / CX-50 / CX-90 see slightly higher premium ($700-$1,200) due to demand; Mazda3 / Mazda6 see lower premium ($500-$900) due to softer sedan market.

So buyer often nets **+$300 to +$900 of value** after paying the CPO premium — the narrowest net-positive among the CPO programs in this reference set. The Mazda CPO math is most easily inverted by aggressive non-CPO pricing.

## What Mazda CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, 12V battery)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, scratched alloys)
- Items modified or altered by aftermarket parts (tunes, lifts, non-Mazda audio, non-Mazda turbos)
- Routine maintenance (no included maintenance under CPO)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at time of CPO inspection
- MX-5 Miata aftermarket coilovers / boost / track modifications (very common on used Miatas — CPO inspection rejects modified units)

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked example — CX-5)

For a 2022 CX-5 Carbon Edition AWD 31k mi:

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **Mazda CPO included** in dealer sale at $26,990 | $26,990 OTD basis (CPO + 7/100k powertrain + 12/12 B2B embedded) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,890 (-$1,100) + buy Mazda Total Confidence Plan $1,900 | $27,790 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,890 + third-party 7/100k powertrain $2,100 | $27,990 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,890 + self-insure | $25,890 net cost, accept ~$1,500-$2,200 of expected-value risk (Mazda reliability statistically reduces expected claim cost vs Hyundai/Ford) |

A non-CPO Mazda priced $800-$1,100 below the CPO equivalent is roughly break-even after self-purchase of comparable coverage. Below $800 differential, CPO wins. The Mazda CPO win-zone is narrower than Hyundai/Subaru CPO win-zones because Mazda's reliability lowers self-insure expected cost.

## Negotiating Mazda CPO (Phase 6 play)

If a Mazda is CPO-eligible but the dealer has not enrolled it:

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Mazda Certified Pre-Owned program as part of the sale? This adds the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty extension and 12mo/12k limited B2B. Standard Mazda-authorized dealer offering for sub-80k mi cars under 6 years from in-service.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for **~$400-$700 internal cost** (150-point inspection + program enrollment fee). They may accept this as part of the deal at no additional charge if OTD ask has been reduced elsewhere.

**Phase 6 negotiation play**: When a non-CPO 2020+ CX-5 / CX-50 / CX-90 is priced within $800 of a CPO same-trim same-mileage comp, ask the non-CPO dealer to enroll CPO at their cost. This is the common Mazda margin give — Mazda dealers tend to be smaller and more flexible than the chain-store Honda/Toyota/Hyundai dealers.

**Mazda3 / Mazda6 specifically**: The sedan market is softer; Mazda CPO premium on sedans often runs only $500-$900. The win-zone for CPO-eligible-but-not-enrolled is narrower; consider whether self-insure or Mazda Total Confidence Plan layered later actually fits the buyer profile better than CPO enrollment.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 6 Years or 80k+ mi)

A 2019 Mazda or any Mazda with 85k+ mi is ineligible for CPO. Options:

1. **Mazda Total Confidence Plan (TCP)** — Mazda's separately-sold extended warranty. Tiers: Major, Comprehensive, Powertrain. Plans 5/60k through 8/100k from time of purchase.
   Typical Mazda dealer TCP pricing for an out-of-CPO 2018 CX-5 (~80k mi at purchase):
   - Comprehensive: approximately $2,400-$3,200
   - Powertrain only: approximately $1,600-$2,200
2. **Third-party extended warranty** — buy elsewhere (CARCHEX / Endurance / Olive). Mazda's reliability often makes third-party plans price aggressively.
3. **Self-insure** — Mazda's reliability statistics (top-5 brand consistently) make this a defensible choice for ICE Mazda with clean maintenance history. The strongest self-insure case among the CPO programs in this reference set.

For out-of-CPO Mazda, expect price to be ~$800 lower than a comparable CPO Mazda (narrower delta than Hyundai/Subaru where the embedded value is higher).

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **Mazda Certified Pre-Owned Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, in-service date
- [ ] **150-point inspection report** (signed by Mazda-certified tech, dated)
- [ ] Verify warranty start date matches original in-service date for powertrain (B2B starts from CPO purchase date)
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation
- [ ] For PHEV (CX-90 PHEV) / Hybrid (CX-50 Hybrid 2025+) / EV (MX-30): verify which components are covered under standard powertrain line — Mazda does not break these out as separate tiers
- [ ] Verify Apple CarPlay / Android Auto functionality (standard since 2018, but verify on used unit — occasional wiring issue on 2018-2019 CX-5)
- [ ] For MX-5 Miata: verify no aftermarket modifications (coilovers / boost kit / track-day mods automatically disqualify CPO)
- [ ] If dealer says "this is CPO" but cannot produce certificate at close: walk or demand documentation

## Brand-Specific Model Notes

### CX-5 (1st gen 2013-2016 retired / 2nd gen 2017-2024 / 3rd gen 2025+)
- Most common Mazda crossover on used market
- 2017-2024 2nd gen powertrains: 2.5L NA / 2.5L Turbo (Signature / Carbon Edition)
- AWD adds ~$1,500-$2,000 over FWD same trim
- Carbon Edition / Signature trims have premium interior — verify trim badging
- 2025+ 3rd gen redesigned — verify generation match

### CX-50 (2023+)
- **Built at MTMUS Huntsville AL** (Mazda Toyota Manufacturing USA JV with Toyota)
- 2.5L NA / 2.5L Turbo / 2.5L Hybrid (2025+, Toyota-derived hybrid powertrain)
- More off-road oriented than CX-5 (Meridian Edition has off-road styling, AWD standard)
- Built-in-Alabama positioning relevant to certain state EV/domestic-content programs (less relevant for ICE)

### CX-90 (2024+) / CX-9 (2016-2023 retired)
- 3-row mid-size SUV
- CX-90 powertrains: 3.3L Turbo inline-6 / 2.5L PHEV (17.8 kWh pack)
- CX-90 PHEV is the highest-value CPO unit for connectivity + hybrid-positioned buyers — but no separate Hybrid Component tier (rolled into powertrain)
- CX-9 (retired 2023) is the predecessor; 2.5L Turbo only; lower second-row passenger space than CX-90

### Mazda3 (4th gen 2019+) / Mazda6 (3rd gen 2014-2021 retired)
- Mazda3 hatchback or sedan; 2.0L / 2.5L / 2.5L Turbo
- Mazda6 retired 2021 — used market only
- Apple CarPlay + Android Auto standard since 2018 — useful Phase 1 anchor

### MX-5 Miata (4th gen "ND" 2016+ / 4.5 facelift 2019+)
- Enthusiast roadster; 2.0L NA inline-4
- ND2 (2019+) bumped HP from 155 to 181
- CPO inspection rejects modified units — Miatas commonly have aftermarket coilovers, track-day items, boost kits
- Resale dynamics dominate — Miata CPO premium often runs ABOVE the embedded warranty value because the model holds value strongly
- For enthusiast buyers: a CPO Miata premium of $1,500-$2,500 over a clean non-CPO comp is often justified by resale persistence, not just the warranty value

### MX-30 EV (2022-2023 retired US market)
- Small BEV (~100 mi EPA range); discontinued in US market 2023
- Very few CPO units on market; if available, verify charging hardware (CCS port) functional
