# Dealer outreach strategy

Research and local composition can proceed without contacting a seller. Before
an external action, prepare the exact vehicle, recipient, channel, message and
attachments, then execute within the buyer's existing authorization.

## Channel selection

Prefer a verified dealer email or the current listing's official contact route.
A lead form is an outbound message and exposes the entered contact information.
Inspect its consent wording and recipients before submission. Never assume an
old site recipe still works or that a contact address belongs to the seller.

Online retailers may use checkout/chat instead of email. Their advertised price
still needs applicable tax, fees, delivery and conditions to establish an OTD.
Verify that the retailer is operating and that the vehicle is available before
relying on historical brand lists.

Private sellers follow the title, lien, inspection and payment steps in
[private-party playbook](private_party_playbook.md). Do not invent dealership
fees or demand a dealer finance process from a private person.

## Deduplication and evidence

Track vehicle VIN/stock ID, dealer/group identity, source channel, authorization,
exact payload, stable operation ID and provider confirmation in private DATA.
If the same VIN appears under different sellers, verify the discrepancy before
contacting both. Multiple stores under common ownership are not automatically
independent competing bids.

A timeout or missing confirmation leaves the operation uncertain. Reconcile
with the provider or seller before resubmission. Do not use a different channel
to replay an uncertain message.

## Message content

Use [email SOP](outbound_email_sop.md) and the
[constrained reply drafter](../../dealer-reply-drafter/SKILL.md).
Request vehicle availability and a complete itemized OTD for the confirmed
registration location. Separate finance, trade and rebate conditions. Protect
the private ceiling. Do not claim cash readiness, preapproval, competing offers,
attachments or urgency without evidence and authorization.

Keep the buyer's preferred contact channel. Phone or SMS observations need a
written follow-up record before they are treated as binding quote evidence.
No reply within a particular number of hours proves neither rejection nor
unavailability; choose follow-up timing from the actual conversation.
