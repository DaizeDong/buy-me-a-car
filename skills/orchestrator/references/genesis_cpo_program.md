# Genesis Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Program (as of 2026-06-22)

> **last_verified**: 2026-06-22 (P3.5 luxury-CPO deliverable; each hard term web-verified against genesis.com/us/en/certified + KBB)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-06-22 timestamp marks last full audit.

Genesis is the luxury arm of Hyundai Motor Group. Its CPO program is a **separate, distinct program** from Hyundai CPO (`hyundai_cpo_program.md`) — shorter eligibility window (5 MY vs 7 yr), tighter mileage cap (60k vs 80k), a deeper inspection (191-point), a **$50 powertrain deductible** (Hyundai is $0), and a comprehensive-warranty line that Hyundai does not match. It sits in the luxury tier alongside BMW / Mercedes-Benz / Lexus CPO, and is documented here rather than in the mainstream 8-brand `cpo-eligibility` matrix.

The single structural standout: a CPO Genesis **reinstates the full 10yr/100k powertrain warranty for the second owner**. On a non-CPO used Genesis, the factory powertrain term shrinks to 5yr/60k on resale (second-owner haircut). CPO restores it to the original 10yr/100k from in-service — a coverage delta that is the core embedded-value driver and is unique among the brands tracked here.

This file mirrors the structure of `hyundai_cpo_program.md`, `subaru_cpo_program.md`, and `toyota_cpo_program.md`.

## Eligibility Criteria

Per Genesis Certified Pre-Owned at genesis.com/us/en/certified (verified 2026-06-22; cross-checked KBB CPO program page):

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age | **5 model years or newer** (e.g., 2026 program covers 2022-2026 model-year vehicles) |
| Mileage | **Fewer than 60,000 miles** |
| Inspection | Must pass Genesis's **191-point** mechanical, safety, and appearance inspection by Genesis-trained technicians |
| Dealer | Must be sold by an authorized Genesis retailer |
| Title / history | Clean title verified by **CARFAX** Vehicle History Report; meets Genesis cosmetic and mechanical reconditioning standards |

A 2023 Genesis GV70 with 28,000 mi sold by a Genesis retailer in 2026 is fully eligible (3 MY / 28k mi — solidly inside both windows).

A 2022 Genesis G80 with 58,000 mi in 2026 is at the eligibility edge: 4 MY old, just under 60k mi — eligible, but verify the odometer at inspection (the 60k mile cap is firm).

A 2021 Genesis (or earlier) in 2026 is generally NOT CPO eligible (older than 5 model years). A 2024 Genesis with 62,000 mi is ineligible on mileage despite being well within the age window — Genesis's 60k cap is the tightest of the Hyundai-group brands (Hyundai/Kia allow 80k).

### Single-tier program

Genesis CPO is a single tier — all eligible units receive the same coverage package. No standard/plus split.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Comprehensive (limited) warranty** | **6 years / 75,000 miles from original in-service date** — extends the factory 5yr/60k new-vehicle B2B by +1 yr / +15k mi |
| **Powertrain limited warranty** | Remainder of the **10 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** (reinstated for the CPO buyer — see below) |
| Powertrain deductible | **$50 per repair visit** |
| Roadside Assistance | **10 years / unlimited mileage**, 24/7/365 (jump start, flat tire, lockout, gas delivery, towing) |
| Rental car reimbursement | **Up to $50/day for up to 10 days** (including 1st-day rental) for covered repairs |
| Trip interruption | Breakdown 150+ mi from home: **up to $100/day, up to $500 per occurrence** (hotel / meals) |
| Vehicle history | Complimentary CARFAX report |
| Connected Services | 3 years Genesis Connected Services |
| SiriusXM trial | 3-month All-Access trial |
| Transferable | Yes — both warranties transfer to the next private owner |

Both the comprehensive and powertrain terms are measured from the **original in-service date and zero miles**, NOT the CPO purchase date.

### The second-owner reinstatement (Genesis-specific structural perk)

On a conventional (non-CPO) used Genesis, the factory powertrain warranty does **not** carry over at full term — it drops to **5yr/60k** for the second owner. The comprehensive coverage similarly does not extend.

Buying a Genesis as **CPO** reinstates the original **10yr/100k powertrain** and adds the **6yr/75k comprehensive** line. Versus a plain used Genesis, the CPO buyer nets roughly **+1 yr / +12k of bumper-to-bumper** and **+4 yr / +40k of powertrain** coverage. This reinstatement is the single highest-value feature of Genesis CPO and the main reason a CPO premium can be justified on this brand.

## Embedded Value

> **Genesis-specific figures — do NOT borrow from mainstream-brand refs.** Genesis is a luxury marque; parts and labor run **2x-4x** the cost of the equivalent Hyundai/Kia repair despite shared platforms. Embedded value is therefore materially higher than the Hyundai-group mainstream brands and is anchored to Genesis's own VSC pricing, not Subaru/Honda/Toyota numbers.

Compare Genesis CPO to the Genesis-dealer-sold extended coverage:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Genesis CPO (built-in) | Already in the dealer's CPO price |
| Genesis Protection Plan VSC — factory extension (e.g., GV80 10yr/100k example) | **~$2,600** (dealer-sold; price varies by model/mileage/term) |
| Third-party luxury VSC (post-CPO, after factory window) | Priced as luxury tier — monthly plans materially above the ~$69/mo non-luxury floor; total typically $3,000-$5,000+ for comparable term |

**Genesis CPO is functionally a ~$2,500-$3,500 embedded value** when the retailer enrolls it as part of the sale. The driver is two-fold: (1) the second-owner powertrain reinstatement (10yr/100k restored vs the 5yr/60k second-owner haircut), and (2) luxury repair costs that make any uncovered powertrain or major-component event expensive — the rotating electronic shift dial (G80/GV80) and the 12.3-inch dual-screen infotainment / cluster are exclusive parts and known cost centers out of warranty.

**Market premium typical**: a CPO Genesis usually carries a premium over an identical non-CPO same-VIN unit; KBB notes dealers tend to charge a high CPO premium and advises negotiating it **below ~$1,000**. When the premium is under ~$1,000, the reinstated coverage delta is strongly in the buyer's favor; above ~$2,000 the value case narrows and the buyer should weigh the Genesis Protection Plan VSC instead.

> **UNVERIFIED**: the precise CPO market price premium (CPO vs same-VIN non-CPO) is dealer- and region-dependent and was not pinned to a single citeable national figure. The "negotiate below $1,000" guidance is KBB editorial, not a published program number. Embedded-value range (~$2.5k-$3.5k) is an estimate anchored to the verified ~$2,600 Genesis VSC factory-extension figure and the verified coverage deltas — treat the point estimate as an estimate, the warranty terms it rests on as verified.

## What Genesis CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, 12V battery)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, curbed wheels)
- Items modified or altered by aftermarket parts (tunes, non-OEM audio, suspension)
- Routine maintenance after the factory term (no included maintenance under CPO)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at the time of CPO inspection
- The **$50 powertrain deductible** applies to each covered powertrain repair visit (this is a cost the buyer bears, unlike Hyundai's $0 deductible)

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked example — Genesis GV70)

For a 2023 Genesis GV70 2.5T AWD, 30k mi:

| Scenario | Effective Coverage / Cost |
|----------|---------------------------|
| **Genesis CPO included** at the dealer's CPO price | 6yr/75k comprehensive + 10yr/100k powertrain from in-service (both reinstated to original term), $50 powertrain deductible |
| Non-CPO same VIN priced ~$1,000 lower | Powertrain drops to second-owner 5yr/60k; no comprehensive extension; full luxury repair exposure after 5yr/60k |
| Non-CPO same VIN ~$1,000 lower + Genesis Protection Plan VSC (~$2,600, must buy before factory B2B expires) | ~$1,600 net worse than CPO, and the VSC must be purchased inside the 5yr/60k factory window |

For a 2022-2026 Genesis under 60k mi, **CPO wins whenever the premium is under ~$1,500-$2,000 over the non-CPO equivalent** — the reinstated 10yr/100k powertrain alone is worth more than that given luxury repair costs. Above ~$2,000 premium, compare against buying the Genesis Protection Plan VSC directly (~$2,600) while still inside the factory window.

## Negotiating Genesis CPO (Phase 6 play)

If a Genesis is CPO-eligible (2022-2026, under 60k mi) but the retailer has not enrolled it:

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Genesis Certified Pre-Owned program as part of the sale? This reinstates the 10yr/100k powertrain to the original in-service term, adds the 6yr/75k comprehensive coverage, the 191-point inspection, and 10yr roadside. Standard authorized-Genesis-retailer offering for sub-60k-mile cars within 5 model years.

KBB explicitly flags that dealers tend to charge a high CPO premium on Genesis — so the negotiation target is to get the premium **below ~$1,000**. The reinstated second-owner powertrain coverage is the leverage point: a non-CPO Genesis loses 5 years / 40k miles of powertrain coverage, so any premium under the value of that delta is a buyer win.

If the retailer will not move the CPO premium under ~$1,500-$2,000, the fallback is to buy the non-CPO car and purchase the **Genesis Protection Plan VSC** (~$2,600) — but only if the car is still inside the 5yr/60k factory bumper-to-bumper window, which is the hard cutoff for buying the dealer VSC.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 5 Model Years or 60k+ mi)

A 2021-or-older Genesis, or any Genesis with 60k+ mi, is ineligible for CPO. Options:

1. **Genesis Protection Plan VSC (Protection Plus)** — Genesis's dealer-sold extended service contract. The factory extension adds up to 5 yr / 60k mi and bumps powertrain mileage from 100k to 120k. **Must be purchased before the factory bumper-to-bumper warranty expires (5 yr / 60k mi)** — this is the hard timing gate. ~$2,600 in the GV80 factory-extension example.
2. **Third-party luxury VSC** — after the factory window closes, third-party providers are the only route (most cover up to ~200k mi). Genesis prices in the luxury tier, so expect rates well above the non-luxury ~$69/mo floor. Verify the authorized-repair network and claim-denial track record before buying.
3. **Self-insure** — bank the premium and pay repairs as they come. Defensible for a 1-owner Genesis with full service history under ~50k mi, but the luxury repair multiplier (2x-4x Hyundai/Kia parts+labor) and known cost centers (electronic shift dial, dual-screen cluster) make this riskier on Genesis than on a mainstream brand.

For an out-of-CPO Genesis, expect price to be meaningfully lower than a comparable CPO car — but factor that the buyer also loses the reinstated 10yr/100k powertrain.

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **Genesis Certified Pre-Owned Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, original in-service date
- [ ] **191-point inspection report** (signed by Genesis-trained tech, dated, pass status per line)
- [ ] Verify the powertrain warranty start date matches the **original in-service date** (not today) — this confirms the reinstatement to 10yr/100k actually applies
- [ ] Verify the **6yr/75k comprehensive** line is explicitly noted (separate from powertrain)
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation
- [ ] Confirm the **$50 powertrain deductible** is disclosed in writing
- [ ] CARFAX clean-title report in hand
- [ ] If the retailer says "this is CPO" but cannot produce the certificate at close: walk or demand documentation before signing

Without these in hand, "Certified" claims are unverified and the buyer may be paying CPO pricing for non-CPO coverage. On a luxury brand the dollar exposure of getting this wrong is larger — verify against Genesis's CRM by VIN before signing.

## Brand-Specific Model Notes

### GV70 (2022+) / GV80 (2021+)
- The two SUVs that dominate Genesis used inventory. GV70 is the compact luxury SUV; GV80 is the mid-size 3-row-optional.
- **Electronic rotating shift dial** on GV80 (and G80) is a known out-of-warranty failure point — the reinstated powertrain/comprehensive coverage materially de-risks this.
- 12.3-inch dual-screen infotainment + instrument cluster are exclusive parts; expensive to replace out of warranty.
- GV70 Electrified (BEV, 2023+) — see EV note below.

### G70 (2019+) / G80 (2021+ current gen) / G90 (2023+ current gen)
- Sport sedan (G70), executive sedan (G80), flagship (G90). All qualify for standard Genesis CPO within the 5MY/60k window.
- Edmunds projects first-post-warranty-year repairs around $845 for G70/G80 — modest, but luxury parts mean a single major event can dwarf that.
- G90 (flagship) carries the highest parts cost; the reinstated coverage is most valuable here.

### Electrified GV70 / Electrified G80 / GV60 (BEV)
- Genesis BEVs (GV60, Electrified GV70, Electrified G80) — the standard Genesis CPO comprehensive + powertrain terms apply.
- **UNVERIFIED**: a separate Genesis CPO high-voltage-battery coverage line (analogous to Hyundai/Kia's 10yr/100k EV battery CPO line) was not confirmed on the genesis.com certified page or KBB at this audit. Genesis's new-vehicle HV battery warranty is 10yr/100k, but whether the **CPO program reinstates that battery line for the second owner separately** is not documented in the verified sources. Verify the CPO certificate's battery-coverage line explicitly before paying any EV CPO premium. Do NOT assume parity with Hyundai/Kia EV CPO battery coverage.

## Genesis CPO vs Hyundai / Kia / Luxury Peers — Quick Comparison

| Feature | Genesis CPO | Hyundai CPO | Kia CPO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier | Luxury | Mainstream | Mainstream |
| Age cap | **5 model years** | 7 yr from in-service | 7 yr from in-service |
| Mileage cap | **60k mi** (tightest in group) | 80k mi | 80k mi |
| Inspection | **191-point** (deepest in group) | 173-point | 165-point |
| Comprehensive (limited) extension | **6yr/75k from in-service** (+1yr/15k over factory) | 1yr/12k post-factory | varies |
| Powertrain | 10yr/100k from in-service (**reinstated for 2nd owner**) | 10yr/100k from in-service | 10yr/100k from in-service |
| Powertrain deductible | **$50/visit** | $0 | $50 |
| Roadside | 10yr / unlimited mi | 10yr / unlimited mi | varies |
| Rental reimbursement | $50/day x 10 days | $35/day x 10 days | varies |
| Trip interruption | $100/day, $500/occurrence | $200/day, $1,000 total | varies |
| Embedded value | **~$2.5k-$3.5k** (luxury repair multiplier) | $1.5k-$2.5k (ICE/Hybrid) | $1.5k-$2.5k |

**Key structural difference**: Genesis CPO is the luxury-tier program in the Hyundai group — tighter eligibility (5MY/60k), deeper inspection (191-pt), a $50 powertrain deductible, and a comprehensive-warranty extension Hyundai/Kia don't match. Its defining feature is the **second-owner reinstatement of the full 10yr/100k powertrain**, which a conventional used Genesis loses (drops to 5yr/60k). Combined with luxury repair costs running 2x-4x the Hyundai/Kia equivalent, that reinstatement makes Genesis CPO the highest-embedded-value program in the Hyundai group on a dollar basis — provided the premium is negotiated under ~$1,500.
