# Mid-cycle pivot protocol

Use this reference when a change to criteria affects active research, quotes or
visit preparations. Work in the verified private cycle directory. Keep previous
revisions and source artifacts in the private companion rather than deleting the
evidence needed to understand an earlier decision.

## Determine what the change affects

| Changed requirement | Recheck |
|---|---|
| Registration location or delivery area | Applicable tax/fees, registration, transport, search coverage and quote comparability |
| Vehicle class, model, trim, mileage or must-have | Candidate eligibility, specifications, market comparison and inspection scope |
| New, used, CPO or seller type | History/delivery documents, warranty, incentives and transaction process |
| Cash, finance, lease, trade or payoff | Itemized acquisition costs, loan/lease terms, conditional discounts and affordability |
| Budget, timeline or intended use | Ranking, private ceiling, availability, quote expiry and visit logistics |

Do not assume every field change invalidates everything, or that a small numerical
change is harmless. A revised towing requirement or must-have can disqualify a
candidate. Changing a private maximum never authorizes changing an outward offer.

## Revise and reconcile

1. Record the changed fact and create a new criteria revision. Carry forward known
   facts that remain valid. Ask one grouped clarification only for material new
   decisions; do not restart the full intake.
2. Hold affected drafts and pending external operations. Continue unaffected
   research. For an operation with uncertain execution, reconcile its provider
   status before any retry. Do not assume a draft was sent or a booking cancelled.
3. Review the private artifact register below. Mark each entry current, partly
   stale, superseded or awaiting verification, with the changed requirement and
   next check. Preserve the previous version and link its replacement.
4. Refresh affected source evidence and recompute the comparison. Retain still
   valid records and their original observation dates; do not make old evidence
   look current by changing only the report date.
5. Update `master_comparison.md`, the buyer research HTML/PDF and relevant visit
   preparations. Deliver the revised package automatically when research is ready.
   Missing written quotes do not block a buyer research report; unresolved costs
   remain visible. Follow [Phase 3](phases.md#phase-3-inventory) and
   [dossier-builder](../../dossier-builder/SKILL.md) for their contracts.
6. If contact is useful, prepare the specific recipient and factual update through
   [dealer-reply-drafter](../../dealer-reply-drafter/SKILL.md). Existing authorization
   remains valid only within its scope. A criteria change does not itself authorize
   new outreach, external draft deletion, cancellation or a purchase commitment.

## Private artifact register

All paths below are relative to the verified private cycle directory. Record the
actual paths used by the cycle; do not create real DATA under the public tool repo.

| Artifact | Recheck after the change | Record |
|---|---|---|
| `criteria.md` and earlier revisions | Changed requirements, unchanged facts, authorization scope | {revision; change; unresolved decisions} |
| Source captures and research notes | Relevance, dates, filters and supported claims | {paths/source IDs; current or affected} |
| `master_comparison.md` | Coverage, candidate eligibility, cost basis and shortlist | {version; sections to refresh} |
| `buyer_research.html` / `buyer_research.pdf` | Conclusions, tables, assumptions and source references | {version; replacement paths; render status} |
| Dealer tracker, quotes and inbox ledger | Active vehicle, registration, financial terms, expiry and delivery status | {record IDs; scope; receipt status} |
| Local or external drafts | Recipient, quoted facts and authorized outward offer | {draft IDs; hold/update plan; no assumed deletion} |
| `negotiation_prep.md` and dealer proposals | Private ladder, visit questions, alternatives and outward terms | {affected sections; proposal validity} |
| Appointments, inspection and delivery plans | Continued relevance, cancellation rules and authorization | {provider receipt; action needed or none} |

At handoff, state what changed, which conclusions remain supported, what is still
uncertain, where the revised buyer package lives and whether any external action
actually occurred. Preserve the boundary between a refreshed research report and
a dealer-ready proposal with complete written financial evidence.
