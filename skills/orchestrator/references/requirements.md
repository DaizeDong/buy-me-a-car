# Requirements and feasibility

Establish only the decisions needed for the current task. Retain confirmed
answers and authorization across sessions. Save real answers in the private
cycle directory using the blank criteria template.

## Purchase inputs

Confirm make/model/trim, years, new/used/CPO class, mileage, must-haves, optional
preferences, search origin/radius and timeline. Ask explicitly whether a budget
means sale price or all-in OTD. Record a private maximum separately from the
outward offer the buyer has authorized, if any.

Confirm where the vehicle will be registered, including local jurisdiction when
tax depends on it. Search ZIP and registration ZIP need not match. Cross-state
buying can change inventory, fees and paperwork; it does not establish a tax
exemption. Verify tax credits for amounts paid elsewhere and the actual DMV
requirements instead of assuming either double tax or automatic exemption.

## Branches

| Branch | Information required |
|---|---|
| Cash | Available funds, accepted instrument, issuer cutoff, independently verified payee and payment instructions |
| Financing | Down payment, amount financed, term, APR, quoted lender fees, preapproval expiry, rebate conditions, prepayment/refinance terms |
| Lease | Selling price, adjusted cap cost, residual, money factor, term, mileage, taxes, acquisition/disposition fees, total due at signing, purchase option |
| Trade | Vehicle/title/condition, independent dated offers, gross allowance, separately dated lien payoff, ownership and same-transaction eligibility |
| EV or PHEV | Charging access, installation/operating cost, actual route/range needs, battery report and transferable warranty, current incentive eligibility |
| Private seller | Seller/title identity, lien release process, payment/title transfer, inspection and state valuation rules; no invented dealer fees |
| Commercial/heavy vehicle | Actual GVWR/use, registration class, financing/insurance, separate current tax advice; ordinary passenger formulas may not apply |

Do not infer financing from a dealer's requested credit application or infer a
trade from ownership of an older car. Ask when the answer materially changes
the analysis.

## Feasibility

Surface the most consequential constraints first: insufficient matching supply,
budget versus current written offers, inspection or financing lead time,
unavailable charging, branded title, missing lien release or unverifiable CPO.
Use observed inventory and actual vendor timing. Do not invent numerical supply
multipliers, guaranteed discounts, standard financing delays or resale penalties.

For CPO, confirm manufacturer participation, VIN-specific certificate, in-service
date and written coverage. For branded titles, obtain actual lender and insurer
acceptability; broad brand-level claims do not decide an individual policy.

## Close-day logistics

Before travel, reconfirm seller availability, test drive/PPI, funding instrument,
issuer cutoff, verified payee, insurance effective date, plate eligibility and
required documents. Ask the DMV/lender/dealer for document requirements; do not
collect SSNs, account numbers or scans of identity documents simply to complete
a checklist.

Explain unfamiliar terms on first use: OTD is the total acquisition price;
F&I is the dealer finance and insurance office; CPO is manufacturer-certified
pre-owned only when verified. Show total financing/lease cost separately from
the purchase price and monthly payment.
