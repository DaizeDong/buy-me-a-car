# Stellantis SPOTiCAR Certified Pre-Owned Program (Ram / Jeep / Chrysler / Dodge / Dodge / Fiat)

> **last_verified**: 2026-06-22 (initial creation; OEM SPOTiCAR/FCA Certified terms cross-checked against KBB)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-06-22 timestamp marks this file's first audit.

SPOTiCAR (formerly "FCA US Certified") is Stellantis's single multi-brand CPO program covering **Chrysler, Jeep, Dodge, Ram, and Fiat** (plus Wagoneer and Alfa Romeo; Maserati excluded) in the U.S. One program, one warranty structure, applied across all five mainstream brands, that is why this is a single file rather than per-brand. Understanding the two-tier structure (top-tier "Certified" vs lower-tier "CPO Go") and the embedded value is critical for negotiations on used Ram 1500, Jeep Grand Cherokee / Wrangler, Dodge Durango / Charger, Chrysler Pacifica, and Fiat 500X.

This file mirrors the structure of `subaru_cpo_program.md` and `honda_cpo_program.md`.

> **Brand-naming caution**: "SPOTiCAR" is the consumer-facing portal/brand. The underlying factory warranty is Mopar's **7-Year/100,000-Mile Powertrain Limited Warranty** + a **3-Month/3,000-Mile Maximum Care** comprehensive layer. Dealers and forums use "FCA Certified", "Stellantis CPO", "CPOV", and "SPOTiCAR" interchangeably for the same program.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

Per SPOTiCAR / FCA Certified (fcacertified.com, spoticar.com) and KBB's Stellantis CPO writeup. The program is **two-tier**:

| Criterion | Top tier ("Certified") | Lower tier ("CPO Go") |
|-----------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Age from model year | **5 model years or newer** | **6 to 10 model years old** |
| Mileage | **Under 75,000 mi** | **75,001, 120,000 mi** |
| Title | Clean title, no frame damage | Clean title |
| Inspection | **125-point** certified inspection | **125-point** certified inspection |
| Dealer | Authorized Stellantis (CJDR/Fiat) dealer | Authorized Stellantis dealer |

**Program-wide outer bound**: no more than **10 model years old** AND no more than **120,000 mi**, clean title, must pass the 125-point inspection. A vehicle outside either tier's window (e.g., 11+ MY or 120k+ mi) is ineligible for any SPOTiCAR certification.

A 2023 Ram 1500 with 35,000 mi at a CJDR dealer in 2026 is **top-tier Certified** eligible.

A 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee with 95,000 mi in 2026 is **CPO Go** eligible (8 MY old, between 75k and 120k mi).

A 2014 Dodge Durango or any Stellantis vehicle with 125,000 mi is **ineligible** (outside the 10-MY / 120k-mi outer bound).

### Tier mechanics that matter for negotiation

- The **125-point inspection is the same** for both tiers, the tier is set by age/mileage, not by a different inspection.
- **Warranty Upgrade eligibility is gated at 74,999 mi**: All FCA US Certified vehicles with the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty and **no more than 74,999 miles** are eligible to add a Certified Upgrade plan (Powertrain Care / Added Care Plus / Maximum Care). CPO Go units already over 75k mi cannot add these upgrades. SRT, diesel, hybrid, Ram Cab/Chassis, and fleet-service vehicles are excluded from upgrades.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

Both tiers grant the same core warranty package:

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Powertrain Limited Warranty** | **7 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** (engine, transmission, driveline) |
| **Maximum Care (comprehensive) Limited Warranty** | **3 months / 3,000 miles**, whichever comes first, starting at CPO sale date OR at expiration of the remaining 3yr/36k factory Basic warranty (whichever is more beneficial to the buyer); covers 5,000+ components |
| Deductible (powertrain) | **$0** |
| Roadside Assistance / Towing | 24-hour "Sign and Go" towing & roadside, **$100 per occurrence** (overage is buyer's responsibility), concurrent with warranty |
| Car Rental Allowance | **Up to $45/day, $225 max per occurrence** (KBB/Stellantis CPO figure). Some Ram-brand pages list $35/day / $175 max, verify per VIN/brand at close. |
| Transferable | Yes, one time to a subsequent buyer; **$150 transfer fee** for the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty |
| CARFAX | Vehicle History Report + buyback guarantee included |
| SiriusXM | 3-month introductory trial |
| Financing | Chrysler Capital available |

> **The single most important caveat, the powertrain clock starts at the ORIGINAL in-service date, not the CPO purchase date.** A 2019 Ram certified in 2026 may have only ~6 months of the 7yr/100k powertrain left, plus the 3mo/3k Maximum Care. It is entirely possible to "buy a CPO Ram that only has the 3-month/3,000-mile Maximum Care warranty remaining." This is the program's biggest value trap, see Embedded Value below.

A 2023 Ram that went into service in 2023 has CPO powertrain coverage through 2030 (7 years) or 100,000 mi, whichever comes first, strong residual coverage. A 2018 unit has effectively only the 3mo/3k bumper layer left.

## Embedded Value

Compare SPOTiCAR CPO to buying equivalent coverage separately. **Note: the embedded dollar value is highly age-dependent because the powertrain term runs from in-service date**, far more so than Subaru/Honda CPO, whose value is more uniform.

Mopar Vehicle Protection (rebranded **FlexCare** in 2026) is the separately-sold extended warranty equivalent. Real 2026 quotes:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| SPOTiCAR CPO (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Mopar/FlexCare Maximum Care (top tier, separate) | ~$1,500,$2,900 typical; up to ~$5,000 for max term/max mileage (8yr/125k) |
| Mopar/FlexCare Powertrain Care Plus (separate, powertrain-focused) | Significantly less than Maximum Care |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance) equivalent | ~$3,000,$3,500 (ConsumerAffairs 2026 Grand Cherokee sample: Mopar undercut third-party) |

**Stellantis-specific embedded value (do NOT cross-apply from Subaru/Honda):**

- On a **top-tier (≤5 MY, <75k mi)** unit with substantial powertrain term remaining, the embedded CPO value is functionally **~$1,000,$1,800**, roughly the cost of the ~2 extra years of powertrain coverage the CPO adds beyond what the original 5yr/60k Mopar powertrain already covered, plus the 3mo/3k comprehensive and roadside.
- On a **CPO Go (6 to 10 MY, 75k to 120k mi)** unit, the embedded value is **much lower (~$300,$800)** because most of the 7yr/100k powertrain is already burned off by age/mileage, the buyer is largely paying for the 3mo/3k Maximum Care + roadside + Carfax.
- **UNVERIFIED**: a single canonical OEM "MSRP value of CPO" figure, Stellantis does not publish one; the ranges above are derived from Mopar/FlexCare quote data, not an OEM list price.

Ram owner consensus corroborates the age-sensitivity: the SPOTiCAR warranty "may add only about 2 years of major powertrain coverage and not much else" on a newer (e.g., 2021) unit that still has substantial factory coverage, so the CPO premium is often not a big deal on near-new units.

**Market premium** (asking-price delta CPO vs identical non-CPO VIN): **UNVERIFIED** as a hard number for Stellantis; budget the embedded-value ranges above as the negotiating anchor rather than a fixed market premium.

## What SPOTiCAR CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, 12V batteries, typical)
- Accident / collision damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, curb-rashed wheels)
- Aftermarket-modified components (tunes, lifts, non-Mopar audio), common on Wrangler/Ram, watch for this
- Routine maintenance (oil changes, fluid services)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at the 125-point inspection
- Non-powertrain components after the 3mo/3k Maximum Care layer expires (this is the big gap, comprehensive coverage is only 90 days)

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked examples)

### Newer / top-tier example, 2023 Ram 1500 Big Horn, 35k mi

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **SPOTiCAR Certified included** at $34,990 | $34,990 basis (CPO value embedded, ~7yr/100k powertrain runs to ~2030) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $33,990 (-$1,000) + buy Mopar Powertrain Care separately | ~$34,990,$35,500 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $33,990 + self-insure | $33,990 net, accept powertrain risk (Ram powertrain claims are not cheap) |

A non-CPO top-tier Ram priced **$1,000+ below** the CPO equivalent is roughly break-even after self-buying comparable powertrain coverage.

### Older / CPO Go example, 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee, 95k mi

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **CPO Go** at $18,990 | $18,990; powertrain warranty **nearly exhausted** (7yr from 2018 ≈ 2025 expiry, or 100k mi imminent), buyer mostly gets 3mo/3k + roadside |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $18,490 (-$500) + self-insure | $18,490 net |

On **CPO Go** units, do NOT pay a meaningful premium for "CPO", the powertrain coverage is often already gone. The certification's real value here is the 125-point inspection + Carfax buyback, not the warranty. Treat a CPO Go premium over $500 skeptically.

## Negotiating SPOTiCAR CPO

If a Stellantis vehicle is CPO-eligible but not yet enrolled (common on recent trade-ins):

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the SPOTiCAR Certified program as part of the sale? For a sub-75k-mile unit under 5 model years that adds the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty, the 3mo/3k Maximum Care, and the 125-point inspection, a standard Stellantis-authorized retailer offering.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for a modest internal cost (inspection + enrollment). On **top-tier** units they may absorb it as part of the deal, especially if you've reduced your OTD ask elsewhere.

**Key plays:**
1. **Top-tier unit**: treat CPO as ~$1,000,$1,800 of embedded value; close the price gap to a non-CPO comp accordingly.
2. **CPO Go unit**: refuse to pay a powertrain-warranty premium, verify the in-service date and compute remaining powertrain term first. If it's <12 months / <10k mi of powertrain left, the "CPO" label is mostly the inspection.
3. **Warranty upgrade**: if the unit is <74,999 mi and you want long-term coverage, you can add a Certified Upgrade (Powertrain Care / Added Care Plus / Maximum Care), but price-shop online sellers (Granger, Zeigler, Riedman, Chrysler Warranty Direct) against the dealer; online is routinely cheaper for the same Mopar/FlexCare plan.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (11+ MY or 120k+ mi)

A 2014 Stellantis vehicle or any with 120k+ mi is ineligible for SPOTiCAR. Options:

1. **Mopar / FlexCare Vehicle Protection (Used Vehicle plan)**, Stellantis's separately-sold extended warranty. Used plans are available if the vehicle is >3 years from its warranty start date or between 36,000 to 80,000 mi (requires a certified-tech inspection). **Vehicles with 80,001+ mi are NOT eligible for any Mopar Vehicle Protection.** This is a hard ceiling, higher-mileage Stellantis vehicles have no OEM-backed extended-warranty path.
2. **Third-party extended warranty** (CARCHEX / Endurance / Olive), the only OEM-ineligible-mileage path; verify claim-payment reputation.
3. **Self-insure**, defensible only with strong maintenance records; note Stellantis reliability is more variable than Honda/Toyota, so self-insuring carries more expected-value risk.

For out-of-CPO units the embedded-value math reverses: expect the price to be modestly lower than a comparable CPO car, but the gap is smaller than for Subaru/Honda because the SPOTiCAR warranty itself is often largely consumed on older units.

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **SPOTiCAR / FCA Certified Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, **original in-service date**
- [ ] **125-point inspection report** (signed by a Stellantis-certified tech)
- [ ] **Compute remaining powertrain term** from the in-service date, this is the load-bearing check on Stellantis CPO (powertrain runs from in-service, not purchase)
- [ ] Confirm which **tier** the unit is (top-tier Certified vs CPO Go), sets warranty-upgrade eligibility
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation + the $150 powertrain transfer fee terms
- [ ] If dealer claims "CPO" but cannot produce certificate at close: walk or demand documentation before signing

Without these in hand, "CPO" claims are unverified and the buyer may be paying CPO pricing for a near-exhausted powertrain warranty.

## Stellantis SPOTiCAR vs Subaru / Honda CPO, Quick Comparison

| Feature | Stellantis SPOTiCAR | Subaru CPO | Honda True Certified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age cap | 10 MY (top tier ≤5 MY) | 5 yr | 6 yr from in-service |
| Mileage cap | 120k mi (top tier <75k) | 80k mi | 85k mi |
| Two-tier program | **Yes (Certified + CPO Go)** | No | Yes (Standard + Certified+) |
| Inspection | 125-point | 152-point | 150-point |
| Powertrain extension | 7yr/100k from in-service | 7yr/100k from in-service | 7yr/100k from in-service |
| Comprehensive layer | 3mo/3k Maximum Care | None | 1yr/12k (std) or 2yr/24k (Certified+) |
| Powertrain deductible | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Embedded value | ~$1,000,$1,800 top-tier; ~$300,$800 CPO Go | $2,000,$2,500 | $1,200,$1,800 |
| Transfer fee | $150 (powertrain) | n/a (free transfer within term) | n/a |

**Key takeaway:** Stellantis SPOTiCAR has the **widest eligibility window** (10 MY / 120k mi) but the **weakest comprehensive layer** (only 3mo/3k vs Honda's 1 to 2 yr) and the **most age-sensitive embedded value** because the powertrain clock runs from in-service. Budget materially less "CPO discount leverage" on CPO Go units than on any Subaru/Honda CPO, and verify the in-service date before treating the powertrain warranty as a real benefit.
