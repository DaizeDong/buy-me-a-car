# Honda Certified Pre-Owned (Honda True Certified) Program

> **last_verified**: 2026-05-18 (skill stress test iteration 5 + P0-P5 consolidation)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-05-18 timestamp marks last full audit.

Honda True Certified is Honda's CPO program. Understanding eligibility and embedded value is critical for negotiations on 2019-2024 Honda CR-V, Accord, Civic, Pilot, Passport, Odyssey, and Ridgeline.

This file mirrors the structure of `subaru_cpo_program.md`.

## Eligibility Criteria

Per Honda Certified Pre-Owned at automobiles.honda.com/certified:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age from original in-service date | **Max 6 years** (e.g., 2026 program covers 2020-2026 in-service vehicles) |
| Mileage | **Under 85,000 mi** |
| Inspection | Must pass Honda's **150-point certified inspection** |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Honda dealer (not independent or off-brand) |
| Title / history | Clean title; no major accident on CARFAX; no flood, salvage, or rebuilt branding |

A 2022 Honda CR-V EX-L with 28,000 mi sold by a Bay Area Honda dealer in 2026 is fully eligible.

A 2019 Honda CR-V is at the eligibility edge: 6 years from in-service ~ early 2025 if vehicle was put into service early 2019; depending on original sale date it may already be ineligible. Verify in-service date with the dealer.

A 2018 Honda is no longer Honda True Certified eligible (>6 years). Honda True USED (the lower tier) may apply but with reduced coverage.

### Two tiers of Honda CPO

Honda has TWO CPO tiers, easy to confuse:

| Tier | Name | Age | Mileage | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top** | **Honda True Certified+** (premium) | <=4 yr in-service | <48k mi | Full premium coverage (see below) |
| **Standard** | **Honda True Certified** | <=6 yr in-service | <85k mi | Standard CPO coverage |
| (No CPO) | **Honda True Used** | >6 yr or >85k mi | n/a | Carfax + 100-day powertrain warranty only; not CPO |

When the scenario is 2020-2023 CR-V <50k mi (the CR-V example), the CR-V is squarely in **Honda True Certified+** tier (the premium tier). When 2019 with 60k mi, it lands in **Honda True Certified** standard tier.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

### Honda True Certified (standard tier)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Powertrain warranty extension** | **7 years / 100,000 miles** from original in-service date |
| **Limited bumper-to-bumper extension** | **1 year / 12,000 miles** after factory 3yr/36k expires (so net: 4yr/48k from in-service for the new owner if factory is still active; or 1yr/12k from CPO purchase if factory has expired) |
| Deductible | $0 |
| Roadside Assistance | 24/7 included, for the duration of any CPO warranty |
| Transferable | Yes (to subsequent buyers within warranty period) |
| Trip Interruption | $300 reimbursement |
| Rental Car Allowance | $35/day for covered repairs (max 6 days) |
| HondaCare Concierge | Some plans include trip planning + emergency services |

The 7yr/100k starts from the original in-service date, NOT the CPO purchase date. So a 2022 Honda that went into service in 2022 has CPO coverage through 2029 (7 years) or 100,000 mi, whichever comes first.

### Honda True Certified+ (premium tier, 4 yr / 48k mi cars)

Adds to the standard tier:
| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Limited bumper-to-bumper extension** | **2 years / 24,000 miles** (vs 1yr/12k on standard tier) |
| 90-day SiriusXM trial | included |
| Honda True Certified+ branding for resale | typical +$300-$500 resale premium |

For the 2022 CR-V scenario, expect Honda True Certified+ (the premium tier, 4 yr old + 28k mi qualifies).

## Embedded Value

Compare Honda CPO to third-party extended powertrain warranties:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Honda True Certified (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Honda Care VSC (separate, post-CPO) | $1,200-$2,200 for additional B2B layered on top |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance) | $1,800-$2,500 for equivalent 7/100k powertrain |
| Liberty Mutual / Geico mechanical breakdown | $1,600-$2,200 |

**Honda True Certified is functionally a $1,200-$1,800 embedded value** when the dealer enrolls it as part of the sale. This is somewhat lower than Subaru CPO ($2,000-$2,500) because Honda's standard factory powertrain warranty (5yr/60k) is shorter than Subaru's, leaving more of the CPO term overlapping with factory coverage.

**Market premium** (asking price) over identical non-CPO same VIN: **$800-$1,500** in Bay Area Honda dealers; ~$500-$1,000 in lower-cost regions. So buyer often nets +$200 to +$700 of value after paying the CPO premium.

## What Honda CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, batteries, typical)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, scratched alloys)
- Items modified or altered by aftermarket parts (tunes, lifts, non-Honda audio)
- Routine maintenance (oil changes, filter services, separate program: HondaCare Sentinel)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at time of CPO inspection

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked example for the CR-V scenario)

For a 2022 CR-V EX-L AWD 28k mi in Bay Area:

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **Honda True Certified+ included** in dealer sale at $26,990 | $26,990 OTD basis (CPO value embedded) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,990 (-$1,000) + buy Honda Care VSC separately at $1,500 | $27,490 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,990 + third-party 7/100k warranty $2,000 | $27,990 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $25,990 + self-insure (accept warranty risk) | $25,990 net cost, accept $1,500-$2,500 of expected-value risk |

A non-CPO Honda priced $1,000 below the CPO equivalent is roughly break-even after self-purchase of comparable coverage. A non-CPO priced $1,500+ below CPO is a small win for self-insuring buyers; below that the CPO unit wins.

## Negotiating Honda CPO

If a Honda is CPO-eligible but the dealer has not enrolled it (sometimes happens with quick trade-in turnaround or recent acquisition):

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Honda True Certified program as part of the sale? This adds the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty extension and 1yr/12k limited B2B and is a standard Honda-authorized retailer offering for sub-85k mi cars under 6 years from in-service.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for **~$400-$700 internal cost** (inspection + program enrollment fee). They may accept this as part of the deal at no additional charge to you, especially if you've reduced your OTD ask elsewhere.

If the dealer charges separately for CPO enrollment ($800-$1,200), it's usually still better value than buying third-party post-sale.

**Phase 6 negotiation play:** When a non-CPO 2020+ CR-V is priced within $500 of a CPO same-trim same-mileage comp, ask the non-CPO dealer to enroll Honda True Certified at their cost as part of the deal. This is the single most-common margin give in Bay Area Honda used-car negotiations.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 6 Years or 85k+ mi)

A 2018 Honda or any Honda with 90k+ mi is ineligible for Honda True Certified. Options:

1. **Honda Care VSC (Vehicle Service Contract)**, separate program (Honda's extended warranty), available at any age/mileage. Plans 5/60k, 6/80k, 7/100k from time of purchase.
   Typical Bay Area Honda dealer Honda Care VSC pricing for a 7-year/100k-mile B2B plan on an out-of-CPO 2018 Honda (~70-80k mi at purchase):
   - Powertrain only: approximately $1,800 to $2,400
   - Comprehensive B2B: approximately $2,800 to $3,500
2. **Third-party extended warranty**, buy elsewhere (CARCHEX / Endurance / Olive)
3. **Self-insure**, accept warranty risk; Hondas are statistically reliable, so this is a defensible choice for 2018+ CR-V / Civic / Accord with clean maintenance history

These are dealer-marked-up. Third-party equivalents may be cheaper but verify the third-party's claim payment reputation.

For out-of-CPO cars, the embedded value math reverses: a buyer should expect the price to be ~$1,000 lower than a comparable CPO car (since they lose that embedded warranty).

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **Honda True Certified Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, in-service date
- [ ] **150-point inspection report** (signed by Honda-certified tech)
- [ ] Verify warranty start date matches original in-service date
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation
- [ ] Verify whether unit is Standard tier or True Certified+ premium tier (different B2B coverage)
- [ ] If dealer says "this is CPO" but cannot produce certificate at close: walk or demand the documentation before signing

Without these in hand, "CPO" claims are unverified and the buyer may be paying CPO pricing for non-CPO coverage.

## Honda CR-V Specific Notes

- 2017-2022 5th gen CR-V is the most common CR-V on Bay Area lots
- 2023-current 6th gen CR-V has different EX-L feature set (different infotainment, new platform), verify trim matches buyer's expectation
- CR-V Hybrid (2020+) is CPO-eligible but premium ~$2,000-$3,000 over non-hybrid same trim
- AWD adds ~$1,500-$2,000 over FWD same trim, year, miles
- Honda CR-V Sport / Sport Touring trims have CPO eligibility but check that Honda Sensing 2.0 / Sensing 360 features match (Sport Touring on 2023+ has Sensing 360 which is a meaningful upgrade)

## Related Honda Programs

- **Honda Care VSC**, Honda's separately-sold extended warranty. Different from CPO.
- **Honda Owner Loyalty Bonus**, for return Honda customers; typically $500-$1,500 off MSRP on new
- **Honda College Graduate Program**, $500 off new (selected models)
- **Honda Military Appreciation**, $500 off new
- **HondaCare Sentinel**, separate prepaid maintenance plan (oil changes, tire rotations)

Most of these apply to NEW Honda purchases. Used Honda True Certified is its own thing, layered on top of the standard sale.

## Honda CPO vs Subaru CPO, Quick Comparison

| Feature | Honda True Certified | Subaru CPO |
|---|---|---|
| Age cap | 6 yr from in-service | 5 yr (current year + 4 preceding) |
| Mileage cap | 85k mi | 80k mi |
| Inspection | 150-point | 152-point |
| Powertrain extension | 7 yr / 100k from in-service | 7 yr / 100k from in-service |
| Limited B2B post-factory | 1 yr / 12k (standard) or 2 yr / 24k (Certified+) | None as standard CPO |
| Embedded value | $1,200-$1,800 | $2,000-$2,500 |
| Market premium typical | $800-$1,500 | $1,500-$2,500 |
| Two-tier program | Yes (Standard + Certified+) | No (single tier) |

Honda CPO embedded value is slightly LOWER than Subaru CPO because Honda factory powertrain (5yr/60k) is shorter than Subaru (5yr/60k same but Honda's CPO 7/100k overlaps less of the gap relative to typical resale age/mileage). Net: budget ~$500-$1,000 less "CPO discount leverage" with Honda than with Subaru.
