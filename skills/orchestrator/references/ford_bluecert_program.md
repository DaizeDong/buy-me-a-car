# Ford Blue Advantage Certified Pre-Owned Program (as of 2026-05-18)

> **last_verified**: 2026-05-18 (skill stress test iteration 5 + P0-P5 consolidation)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-05-18 timestamp marks last full audit.

Ford Blue Advantage is Ford's two-tier CPO program, structurally unique among the major US OEM CPO programs for offering **two distinct coverage tiers (Gold and Blue)** that map to different eligibility windows. This dual-tier model resembles Honda's Standard/Certified+ split but the Ford gap is structurally larger (Gold ≈ Toyota TCUV depth; Blue ≈ thin warranty wrap with no factory backing).

For pickup-truck buyers (F-150, F-250, F-350, Ranger, Maverick), the program is layered with engine-specific coverage on PowerStroke diesel models. For Ford EVs (F-150 Lightning, Mustang Mach-E, E-Transit), Gold tier adds 8yr/100k high-voltage battery coverage, but only Gold; Blue tier does NOT cover EVs.

This file mirrors the structure of `subaru_cpo_program.md`, `honda_cpo_program.md`, `toyota_cpo_program.md`, `hyundai_cpo_program.md`, and `kia_cpo_program.md`.

## Eligibility Criteria

Per Ford Blue Advantage at fordbluecertified.com:

### Gold Certified tier (top, factory-backed)

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age from original in-service date | **Max 6 years** (e.g., 2026 program covers 2020-2026 in-service vehicles) |
| Mileage | **Under 80,000 mi** |
| Inspection | Must pass Ford's **172-point Gold Certified inspection** |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Ford dealer |
| Title / history | Clean title; no major accident on CARFAX / AutoCheck; no flood, salvage, fire, or rebuilt branding |
| Factory backing | Yes, full Ford factory-backed warranty extensions |

### Blue Certified tier (entry, dealer-backed, broader pool)

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age from original in-service date | **Max 10 years** |
| Mileage | **Under 120,000 mi** |
| Inspection | Must pass Ford's **139-point Blue Certified inspection** (more limited) |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Ford dealer |
| Title / history | Clean title; no flood/salvage/fire/rebuilt; minor accident history allowed if documented and repaired |
| Factory backing | Limited, Ford-administered but coverage is dealer-financial-backed for non-original-warranty terms |

### Critical buyer-side distinction

**Gold ≠ Blue.** When a Ford dealer advertises a vehicle as "Ford Blue Advantage Certified" without further qualification, ASK WHICH TIER. The structural coverage gap is enormous:

- A Gold-certified 2022 F-150 has 7yr/100k powertrain + 12mo/12k limited B2B from in-service.
- A Blue-certified 2018 F-150 has 90day/4k limited only, essentially a "lightly-inspected used car with branding" not a factory-backed CPO.

This is the single most-common source of buyer confusion in Ford CPO negotiations. The "Blue" name reads as the marquee program (Ford's blue oval), but Blue is actually the lower tier.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

### Gold Certified (top tier)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Powertrain warranty extension** | **7 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** |
| **Limited bumper-to-bumper extension** | **12 months / 12,000 miles** added (from CPO purchase date, NOT from original in-service) |
| **Comprehensive Limited Warranty** | Includes engine, transmission, drive axles, transfer case, suspension, electrical |
| Deductible | $100 per visit on covered repairs |
| Roadside Assistance | 12 months / unlimited mi included with Gold |
| Transferable | Yes (one transfer allowed) |
| Trip Interruption | $75/day, up to $500 total |
| Rental Car Allowance | $30/day for covered repairs (max 10 days) |
| Sirius XM trial | 90-day on enrollment |

### Blue Certified (entry tier)

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Limited Warranty** | **90 days / 4,000 miles** from CPO purchase date (no extension to factory term) |
| Inspection | 139-point inspection only |
| Deductible | $100 per visit |
| Roadside Assistance | 90 days included |
| Transferable | No |
| Trip Interruption | None |

Blue tier is functionally a "dealer warranty with inspection", comparable to most independent-dealer 90-day used-car warranties. The Ford branding adds marketing value but minimal coverage value beyond what an independent dealer might offer.

### Pickup / PowerStroke Diesel coverage (Gold + Blue, but Gold preferred)

Pickup-truck-specific coverage for F-150 / F-250 / F-350 / Ranger / Maverick on **Gold Certified**:

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| Same Gold powertrain + B2B as above | (applies to all Gold pickups) |
| **PowerStroke Diesel coverage** (6.7L on F-250/F-350; 3.0L Power Stroke on F-150 2018-2021) | **7 years / 100,000 miles from in-service**, diesel-specific tier including fuel injectors, high-pressure fuel pump, turbo, DEF system, EGR cooler, particulate filter (DPF) |
| **EcoBoost twin-turbo coverage** (2.7L / 3.5L EcoBoost on F-150) | Standard Gold powertrain coverage applies; no separate EcoBoost tier (though dealers sometimes upsell separate EcoBoost extended warranty) |
| Towing-package electronics | Integrated Trailer Brake Controller (TBC) covered under B2B |

PowerStroke diesel coverage is the structural premium-tier value on F-250/F-350. Out-of-warranty PowerStroke 6.7L injector replacement runs **$4,500-$7,000**; high-pressure fuel pump failure (with collateral damage when it goes) runs **$8,000-$12,000**. The 7yr/100k diesel-specific coverage on a 3-4 year old F-250 with 50-70k mi has very real residual value.

Blue tier pickups get NO diesel-specific coverage, only the 90day/4k generic B2B.

### Ford EV coverage (F-150 Lightning / Mustang Mach-E / E-Transit), Gold ONLY

EV models are **Gold-tier eligible only**. Blue tier excludes EVs.

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| Standard Gold 7yr/100k powertrain + 12/12 B2B | Applies |
| **High-voltage battery coverage** | **8 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** (matches federal EV mandate; Ford does not exceed federal floor) |
| Drive motor / inverter / onboard charger | Covered under HV battery component line |
| BlueCruise hands-free driving feature | Covered under B2B (sensor/camera/radar) |
| Mobile Power Cord (L1/L2 portable) | Covered |

Ford's 8yr/100k HV battery coverage is shorter than Hyundai/Kia's 10yr/100k EV battery coverage. This is a structural disadvantage for Ford EV CPO vs Hyundai Ioniq 5 / Kia EV6 CPO comparisons.

## Embedded Value

Compare Ford Blue Advantage to third-party extended powertrain warranties:

### Gold tier embedded value

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Ford Gold Certified (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Ford ESP (Extended Service Plan, separate post-CPO) | $1,800-$3,500 layered on top |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance, Olive) | $2,000-$3,000 for equivalent 7/100k powertrain |
| Third-party diesel-inclusive coverage | $3,000-$4,500 (limited providers cover diesel) |
| Third-party EV-battery-inclusive coverage | $3,500-$5,500 |

**Gold Certified is functionally a $1,500-$2,500 embedded value for ICE/Hybrid models** when the dealer enrolls it. For PowerStroke diesel, embedded value rises to **$2,500-$4,000**. For Ford EVs (Lightning, Mach-E), embedded value is **$2,000-$3,500** (lower than Hyundai/Kia EV CPO because Ford only matches federal 8/100k mandate vs Hyundai/Kia's 10/100k).

**Market premium typical for Gold**: **$1,000-$1,800 over identical non-CPO same VIN** at typical US Ford dealers.

### Blue tier embedded value

**Blue Certified is functionally a $400-$800 embedded value**, barely above a private-party sale on a 7-10 year old, 80-120k mi vehicle. The 90day/4k coverage is minimal.

**Market premium typical for Blue**: **$200-$500 over non-Blue equivalent**. The Ford branding adds marketing trust but limited coverage value.

**Critical Phase 6 implication**: Pay no more than $500 premium for Blue Certified over a comparable non-certified Ford. Anything more is dealer margin theater for branding.

## What Ford CPO Does NOT Cover (Both Tiers)

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, 12V battery)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, scratched alloys, bed liner wear on pickups)
- Items modified or altered by aftermarket parts (tunes, lifts, non-Ford audio, non-Ford turbos)
- Routine maintenance (no included maintenance under CPO)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at time of inspection
- Blue tier explicitly: no factory warranty extension, no B2B beyond 90day/4k, no diesel-specific tier, no EV coverage

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked example, F-150 PowerStroke)

For a 2022 F-150 Platinum 3.0L PowerStroke Diesel 48k mi:

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **Ford Gold Certified included** in dealer sale at $54,990 | $54,990 OTD basis (Gold + 7/100k powertrain + 12/12 B2B + diesel-specific tier embedded) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $53,490 (-$1,500) + buy Ford ESP $3,200 (PremiumCARE + diesel rider) | $56,690 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $53,490 + third-party 7/100k powertrain only $2,400 (no diesel-specific) | $55,890 effective with diesel risk uncovered ($4,500-$12,000 expected-value exposure on injectors/HPFP) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $53,490 + self-insure | $53,490 net cost, accept ~$6,000-$10,000 expected-value risk on diesel-specific components |

For a PowerStroke diesel F-150 or F-250/F-350, Gold Certified is structurally hard to beat with self-insure math. The diesel-specific component risk profile is the dominant variable.

## Negotiating Ford Blue Advantage (Phase 6 play)

If a Ford is Gold-eligible but the dealer has not enrolled it:

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Ford Blue Advantage Gold Certified program as part of the sale? This adds the 7yr/100k powertrain warranty extension, 12mo/12k limited B2B, and (for PowerStroke diesel models) the diesel-specific component coverage. Standard Ford-authorized dealer offering for sub-80k mi cars under 6 years from in-service.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for **~$600-$900 internal cost** (172-point inspection + program enrollment fee). They may accept this as part of the deal at no additional charge if OTD ask has been reduced elsewhere.

**Phase 6 negotiation play for Blue tier specifically**: When a Ford is marketed as "Ford Blue Advantage Certified" but is Blue tier only (>6 yr or >80k mi), do NOT pay Gold-tier premium. The buyer challenge:

> Is this Gold Certified or Blue Certified? If Blue, the coverage is 90 days / 4,000 miles, comparable to a standard dealer 90-day warranty. I'd value Blue branding at $300-$500 over non-certified, not $1,500.

**Phase 6 negotiation play for Gold pickups (PowerStroke)**: When a non-Gold PowerStroke diesel is priced within $2,000 of a Gold same-model same-mileage comp, ask the non-Gold dealer to enroll Gold Certified at their cost. The diesel-specific component coverage delta makes this the highest-leverage margin give on Ford diesel pickup negotiations.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 10 Years or 120k+ mi)

A 2014 Ford or any Ford with 125k+ mi is ineligible for both Gold and Blue. Options:

1. **Ford Extended Service Plan (ESP)**, Ford's separately-sold extended warranty. Plans 5/60k, 7/100k, 8/125k from time of purchase. PowerStroke Diesel-specific tiers available. EV-specific tiers for Lightning / Mach-E.
   Typical Ford dealer ESP pricing for an out-of-CPO 2015 F-150 EcoBoost (~110k mi at purchase):
   - PremiumCARE comprehensive: approximately $2,800-$3,800
   - Powertrain only: approximately $1,800-$2,500
   - Diesel-specific rider (F-250/F-350): +$700-$1,200
2. **Third-party extended warranty**, buy elsewhere (CARCHEX / Endurance / Olive). For PowerStroke diesel, verify the plan explicitly covers injectors, high-pressure fuel pump, turbo, DPF, DEF system. Most generic plans EXCLUDE diesel-specific components.
3. **Self-insure**, Ford reliability varies by powertrain. EcoBoost 3.5L has known cam phaser issues on early years; PowerStroke 6.7L has CP4 fuel pump risk. Self-insure is defensible for 2.7L EcoBoost or normally-aspirated V8 with clean maintenance history.

For out-of-CPO Ford pickups, expect price to be ~$1,500-$2,500 lower than a comparable Gold-certified pickup. For diesel out-of-CPO, expect ~$3,000-$5,000 lower.

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **Ford Blue Advantage Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, in-service date, and **explicit tier marker (Gold vs Blue)**
- [ ] **172-point Gold inspection report** OR **139-point Blue inspection report** (signed by Ford-certified tech, dated), match the tier
- [ ] Verify warranty start date matches original in-service date (Gold only, Blue B2B starts from CPO purchase date)
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation (Gold only, Blue is non-transferable)
- [ ] For PowerStroke diesel: verify diesel-specific component coverage is explicitly noted on the Gold certificate
- [ ] For EV models: verify 8yr/100k HV battery coverage is explicitly noted (Gold only; Blue does not cover EVs)
- [ ] Verify $100 deductible disclosure (Ford uses $100/visit, different from Hyundai/Toyota/Honda/Subaru $0)
- [ ] If dealer says "this is Certified" without specifying tier, demand explicit Gold/Blue tier on documentation before signing

## Brand-Specific Model Notes

### F-150 (14th gen 2021+ / 13th gen 2015-2020)
- Most common Ford pickup on used market
- 14th gen powertrains: 3.3L V6 / 2.7L EcoBoost / 5.0L V8 / 3.5L EcoBoost / 3.5L PowerBoost Hybrid / 3.0L PowerStroke Diesel (2018-2021 only) / Electric Lightning
- 13th gen: similar but 3.3L Cyclone V6 in early years
- AWD/4WD adds ~$2,500-$4,000 over RWD same trim
- Tow Package (Max Tow on Lariat+) verification critical, see `vertical_playbooks.md#part-1-pickup-truck-specifics` § 4

### Super Duty F-250 / F-350 (3rd gen 2017-2022 / 4th gen 2023+)
- 6.2L V8 gas / 7.3L "Godzilla" V8 gas (2020+) / 6.7L PowerStroke Diesel
- 6.7L PowerStroke is the high-value variant, diesel-specific Gold coverage critical
- Trim: XL / XLT / Lariat / King Ranch / Platinum / Limited / Tremor

### Ranger (4th gen 2024+ / 3rd gen 2019-2023)
- Mid-size pickup; 2.3L EcoBoost or 2.7L EcoBoost (Raptor)
- Gold Certified applies; no diesel option in US Ranger market

### Maverick (2022+)
- Compact pickup; 2.5L Hybrid (standard) or 2.0L EcoBoost (optional)
- Hybrid powertrain qualifies for standard Gold powertrain coverage (no separate Hybrid Component tier like Toyota TCUV)

### Bronco (6th gen 2021+) / Bronco Sport (2021+)
- Bronco: 2.3L / 2.7L EcoBoost; Bronco Sport: 1.5L / 2.0L EcoBoost
- Trim stratification matters: Base / Big Bend / Black Diamond / Outer Banks / Badlands / Wildtrak / Heritage / Raptor

### Explorer (6th gen 2020+) / Expedition (4th gen 2018-2024 / 5th gen 2025+)
- Mid-size and full-size 3-row SUVs
- Explorer ST (high-performance) and King Ranch / Platinum verify trim
- Expedition Max is long-wheelbase variant

### Mustang (7th gen 2024+ / 6th gen 2015-2023)
- Coupe / convertible; 2.3L EcoBoost / 5.0L Coyote V8 / Mach 1 (retired 2023) / GT500 (retired 2023) / Dark Horse (2024+)
- Mustang Mach-E is a SEPARATE EV crossover, not part of the Mustang coupe lineage

### F-150 Lightning (2022+) / Mustang Mach-E (2021+) / E-Transit (2022+), EVs
- Gold-only CPO; 8/100k HV battery coverage
- Lightning Extended Range pack (131 kWh) vs Standard Range (98 kWh), verify pack size matches advertised range
- Mach-E Extended Range RWD / AWD / GT Performance, battery pack size varies
- See `ev_buyer_playbook.md` for SoH and NACS adapter notes

| Feature | Ford Gold | Ford Blue | Toyota TCUV | Hyundai CPO | Kia CPO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Age cap | 6 yr from in-service | 10 yr from in-service | 6 yr | 7 yr | 5 yr |
| Mileage cap | 80k mi | 120k mi | 85k mi | 80k mi | 60k mi |

| **Diesel-specific coverage** | **Yes, 7/100k diesel tier (PowerStroke)** | No | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| EV HV battery coverage | 8 yr / 100k (Gold only) | n/a (Blue excludes EVs) | n/a (separate bZ4X BEV warranty) | 10 yr / 100k | 10 yr / 100k |

| Embedded value (diesel) | $2,500-$4,000 | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Market premium typical | $1,000-$1,800 | $200-$500 | $1,000-$2,000 | $800-$1,500 | $700-$1,500 |
| Tier structure | **Two-tier (Gold + Blue)** | (lower tier) | Single tier | Single tier | Single tier |

**Key structural differences**: Ford is the only major US OEM with a two-tier CPO + a dedicated diesel-specific component tier. Gold is competitive with Toyota TCUV / Hyundai CPO on ICE; Blue is a marketing-grade designation, not a real factory-backed CPO. PowerStroke diesel buyers should treat Gold as mandatory; ICE F-150 / Ranger / Maverick buyers should compare Gold premium against the standard Ford ESP cost.
