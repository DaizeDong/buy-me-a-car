# Kia Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Program (as of 2026-05-18)

> **last_verified**: 2026-05-18 (skill stress test iteration 5 + P0-P5 consolidation)
> **Data refresh schedule**: state rates / CPO programs / EV incentives / lease parameters should be re-verified annually or upon any user-cited deal that contradicts. The 2026-05-18 timestamp marks last full audit.

Kia CPO shares the Hyundai-Motor-Group powertrain warranty structure — both inherit the 10yr/100k "America's Best Warranty" framing, which is the longest industry-standard powertrain term among non-luxury OEMs.

Kia CPO is structurally similar to Hyundai CPO but uses tighter eligibility thresholds (5 years vs 7 years, 60k mi vs 80k mi, 165-point inspection vs 173-point) and slightly lower embedded value at the median.

For Kia EVs (EV6, Niro EV, EV9), the CPO adds the same separately-tracked 10yr/100k high-voltage battery component coverage as Hyundai — strongest single EV-CPO line item among non-luxury brands.

Hybrid models (Sorento Hybrid, Sportage Hybrid, Niro Hybrid, Carnival Hybrid 2025+) get a separate 8yr/100k Hybrid Component coverage line — distinct from the powertrain line.

This file mirrors the structure of `subaru_cpo_program.md`, `honda_cpo_program.md`, `toyota_cpo_program.md`, and `hyundai_cpo_program.md`.

## Eligibility Criteria

Per Kia Certified Pre-Owned at kia.com/us/en/certified-pre-owned:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Age from original in-service date | **Max 5 years** (e.g., 2026 program covers 2021-2026 in-service vehicles) |
| Mileage | **Under 60,000 mi** |
| Inspection | Must pass Kia's **165-point certified inspection** |
| Dealer | Must be sold by authorized Kia dealer |
| Title / history | Clean title; no major accident on CARFAX / AutoCheck; no flood, salvage, fire, or rebuilt branding |

A 2023 Kia Sportage EX with 28,000 mi sold by a Kia dealer in 2026 is fully eligible (3 yr / 28k mi — solidly inside both windows).

A 2021 Kia Telluride with 58,000 mi in 2026 is at the eligibility edge: 5 yr from in-service if early-2021 build, under 60k mi — eligible. A 2021 Telluride with 62,000 mi is OVER the mileage cap and ineligible.

A 2020 Kia in 2026 is generally NOT CPO eligible (>5 years from in-service).

Note: Kia's eligibility window is **tighter than Hyundai's** (5/60k vs 7/80k) despite sharing the corporate parent. This is a deliberate Kia North America positioning choice (newer/lower-mileage units only).

### Single-tier program

Kia CPO is a single tier — no Standard/Premium split like Honda True Certified. All eligible Kia units get the same coverage package.

## Coverage Granted (When Enrolled)

### ICE models

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Powertrain warranty extension** | **10 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** (longest in industry alongside Hyundai) |
| **Limited bumper-to-bumper extension** | **1 year / 12,000 miles** added after factory 5yr/60k B2B expires |
| Deductible | $50 (note: this is higher than Hyundai's $0) |
| Roadside Assistance | 10 years / unlimited mi 24/7 included (from original in-service date) |
| Transferable | Yes (to subsequent buyers within warranty period) |
| Trip Interruption | $35/day, up to $250 total |
| Rental Car Allowance | $35/day for covered repairs (max 10 days) |
| Sirius XM trial | 90-day on enrollment |

The 10yr/100k powertrain coverage starts from the **original in-service date**, NOT the CPO purchase date.

### Hybrid models (Sorento Hybrid / Sportage Hybrid / Niro Hybrid / Carnival Hybrid)

Adds to the ICE coverage:

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **Hybrid Component coverage** | **8 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** |
| Hybrid battery, hybrid motor, hybrid control unit, inverter | All covered under Hybrid Component line |
| Hybrid coolant pump, traction battery thermal management | Covered |

Note: Kia's Hybrid Component coverage is **8/100k**, shorter than Toyota TCUV's **10/150k** Hybrid Component coverage. Toyota wins on hybrid-specific term length; Kia wins on ICE powertrain term length.

### EV models (EV6 / Niro EV / EV9)

EV models get the standard ICE coverage PLUS a separate high-voltage battery component coverage line:

| Coverage | Term |
|----------|------|
| **High-voltage battery component coverage** | **10 years / 100,000 miles from original in-service date** |
| Battery degradation threshold | If pack falls below 70% state-of-health within 10yr/100k window, Kia covers replacement |
| Onboard charger / inverter / electric motor | Covered under HV battery component line |
| Reduction gear (EV transaxle) | Covered under powertrain warranty (10yr/100k) |

This matches Hyundai's EV CPO coverage line item. Out-of-warranty EV6 / EV9 battery replacement runs **$16,000-$24,000** at a Kia dealer. The 10yr/100k CPO coverage on a 2-4 year old EV6 with 25-50k mi has very real residual value.

## Embedded Value

Compare Kia CPO to third-party extended powertrain warranties:

| Coverage Source | Cost |
|-----------------|------|
| Kia CPO (built-in) | Already in dealer price |
| Kia Protection Plan VSA (separate, post-CPO) | $1,400-$2,500 layered on top |
| Third-party (CARCHEX, Endurance, Olive) | $1,800-$2,800 for equivalent 10/100k powertrain |
| Third-party EV-battery-inclusive coverage | $3,500-$5,500 (very limited provider availability) |

**Kia CPO is functionally a $1,200-$2,000 embedded value for ICE models** when the dealer enrolls it as part of the sale. Slightly lower than Hyundai CPO ($1,500-$2,500) because Kia's tighter eligibility windows mean fewer "deep coverage value" candidates — most CPO Kias are 2-4 years old with 20-45k mi, where the marginal warranty value over factory is smaller.

**For EV models (EV6, Niro EV, EV9), embedded value rises to $2,800-$4,200** — slightly less than Hyundai EV CPO ($3,000-$4,500) at the median, primarily because EV6 / EV9 are newer models with fewer high-mileage CPO candidates on the market.

**Market premium typical**: **$700-$1,500 over identical non-CPO same VIN** at typical US Kia dealers. EV CPO premium often runs an additional $200-$500.

So buyer often nets **+$500 to +$1,300 of value** (ICE) or **+$1,800 to +$3,200** (EV) after paying the CPO premium.

## What Kia CPO Does NOT Cover

- Wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades, 12V battery)
- Accident damage (not warranty work)
- Cosmetic items (paint chips, interior wear, scratched alloys)
- Items modified or altered by aftermarket parts (tunes, lifts, non-Kia audio)
- Routine maintenance (no included maintenance under CPO — separate Kia Care Maintenance Plan available for purchase)
- Pre-existing conditions documented at time of CPO inspection
- 12V auxiliary battery on EVs (only HV traction pack is covered under EV battery line)
- The $50 deductible applies per visit on B2B claims (not powertrain — powertrain remains $0)

## CPO vs Non-CPO Math (worked example — Sorento Hybrid)

For a 2023 Kia Sorento Hybrid EX 28k mi:

| Scenario | Effective Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| **Kia CPO included** in dealer sale at $34,990 | $34,990 OTD basis (CPO + 10/100k powertrain + 8/100k hybrid + 1/12 B2B embedded) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $33,490 (-$1,500) + buy Kia Protection Plan VSA $2,200 + hybrid third-party rider $1,200 | $36,890 effective |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $33,490 + third-party 10/100k powertrain only $2,400 (no hybrid component coverage) | $35,890 effective with hybrid risk uncovered ($3,500-$5,500 expected-value exposure) |
| Non-CPO same VIN at $33,490 + self-insure | $33,490 net cost, accept ~$4,000-$6,000 of expected-value risk on hybrid + powertrain combined |

A non-CPO Kia Hybrid priced $1,500 below the CPO equivalent is roughly break-even on ICE-equivalent coverage — but the Hybrid Component coverage gap hurts hybrid buyers. **For Kia Hybrid models, CPO is hard to beat with self-insure math.**

## Negotiating Kia CPO (Phase 6 play)

If a Kia is CPO-eligible but the dealer has not enrolled it:

> Could you enroll this vehicle in the Kia Certified Pre-Owned program as part of the sale? This adds the 10yr/100k powertrain warranty extension, 1yr/12k limited B2B, and (for EV models) the 10yr/100k high-voltage battery coverage. Standard Kia-authorized dealer offering for sub-60k mi cars under 5 years from in-service.

Dealers can enroll a qualifying car for **~$500-$800 internal cost** (165-point inspection + program enrollment fee). They may accept this as part of the deal at no additional charge, especially if OTD ask has been reduced elsewhere.

If the dealer charges separately for CPO enrollment ($1,000-$1,500), it's usually still better value than buying third-party post-sale — especially for EV models.

**Phase 6 negotiation play**: When a non-CPO 2022+ EV6 / EV9 / Niro EV is priced within $1,500 of a CPO same-trim same-mileage comp, ask the non-CPO dealer to enroll CPO at their cost as part of the deal. The HV battery coverage delta makes this the highest-leverage margin give on Kia EV used-car negotiations.

## For Out-of-CPO Cars (Over 5 Years or 60k+ mi)

A 2020 Kia or any Kia with 65k+ mi is ineligible for CPO. Options:

1. **Kia Protection Plan (KPP)** — Kia's separately-sold extended warranty. Plans 5/60k, 7/100k, 10/100k from time of purchase. For EV models, separate EV Protection tier covering HV battery components.
   Typical Kia dealer KPP pricing for an out-of-CPO 2020 Sportage (~70k mi at purchase):
   - Powertrain only: approximately $1,700-$2,400
   - Comprehensive B2B: approximately $2,600-$3,600
   - Comprehensive B2B + EV battery (EV only): approximately $3,800-$5,200
2. **Third-party extended warranty** — buy elsewhere (CARCHEX / Endurance / Olive). For Kia EVs, verify third-party plan explicitly covers HV traction battery, inverter, onboard charger.
3. **Self-insure** — accept warranty risk. Kia's reliability has improved alongside Hyundai's; 2020+ Kia with clean maintenance history is defensible self-insure. **NOT recommended for out-of-warranty Kia EV** — the HV battery replacement event is the structural risk.

For out-of-CPO ICE Kia, expect price to be ~$1,000 lower than a comparable CPO car. For out-of-CPO Kia EV, expect ~$2,500-$3,800 lower.

## CPO Verification at Close

Confirm CPO status before signing:

- [ ] **Kia Certified Pre-Owned Certificate** (PDF) with VIN, vehicle data, in-service date
- [ ] **165-point inspection report** (signed by Kia-certified tech, dated)
- [ ] Verify warranty start date matches original in-service date
- [ ] Confirm transferability documentation
- [ ] For Hybrid models: verify 8yr/100k Hybrid Component coverage is explicitly noted on the CPO certificate (separate from powertrain)
- [ ] For EV models: verify HV battery component coverage 10yr/100k is explicitly noted (separate line from powertrain)
- [ ] Verify the $50 B2B deductible disclosure (some buyers expect Hyundai's $0; the $50 is a Kia-specific deduct)
- [ ] If dealer says "this is CPO" but cannot produce certificate at close: walk or demand documentation before signing

## Brand-Specific Model Notes

### Telluride (1st gen 2020+)
- Most-demanded Kia on used market; rarely discounted heavily
- 2020-2023 Telluride uses Lambda II V6; well-regarded reliability
- Trim stratification: LX / S / EX / SX / SX-Prestige / X-Line / X-Pro — verify trim badging matches advertised price ($6k-$10k spread)
- Telluride does not have a Hybrid trim (yet — 2026 model year may introduce one)

### Sorento Hybrid / Sorento PHEV (4th gen 2021+)
- Mid-size 3-row SUV with hybrid options
- Sorento Hybrid 8/100k Hybrid Component coverage applies on CPO
- Sorento PHEV (2022+) uses larger pack — same 8/100k Hybrid Component but bigger replacement cost ($4,500-$6,500)

### Sportage / Sportage Hybrid (5th gen 2023+)
- Compact SUV; Hybrid trims qualify for 8/100k Hybrid Component
- 5th gen completely redesigned vs 4th gen — verify generation match

### Niro Hybrid / Niro PHEV / Niro EV (2nd gen 2023+)
- Subcompact crossover with three powertrain options; all qualify for relevant CPO coverage lines
- Niro EV qualifies for 10/100k HV battery coverage

### EV6 / EV9 (2022+ / 2024+)
- EV6 is compact crossover EV; EV9 is full-size 3-row EV
- Both use 800V architecture — supports 250kW+ DC fast charging
- EV6 GT (2023+) is high-performance trim — qualifies for CPO but verify aftermarket-tune-clean before purchase
- See `ev_buyer_playbook.md` for SoH check protocol and NACS adapter notes

### K5 (formerly Optima) / Forte / Soul
- Sedan / hatchback / subcompact crossover; standard CPO coverage
- K5 GT-Line / GT trims are performance-oriented; verify tune integrity

### Carnival (4th gen 2022+)
- Minivan; Carnival Hybrid arrives 2025 model year — Hybrid CPO will apply
- Current Carnival uses 3.5L V6; standard ICE CPO coverage

## Related Kia Programs

- **Kia Protection Plan (KPP)** — Kia's separately-sold extended warranty / VSA. Different from CPO.
- **Kia Care Maintenance Plan** — paid prepaid maintenance plan (oil changes, tire rotations)
- **Kia Owner Loyalty** — return Kia customer; typically $500-$1,000 off MSRP on new
- **Kia College Graduate / Military** — $400-$500 off new

Most of these apply to NEW Kia purchases. Used Kia CPO is its own thing.

## Kia CPO vs Hyundai CPO vs Toyota TCUV vs Honda CPO vs Subaru CPO — Quick Comparison

| Feature | Kia CPO | Hyundai CPO | Toyota TCUV | Honda True Certified | Subaru CPO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Mileage cap | **60k mi** (tightest) | 80k mi | 85k mi | 85k mi | 80k mi |
| Inspection | 165-point | 173-point | 160-point | 150-point | 152-point |
| Powertrain extension | **10 yr / 100k from in-service** (longest, tied with Hyundai) | 10 yr / 100k | 7 yr / 100k | 7 yr / 100k | 7 yr / 100k |
| Limited B2B post-factory | 1 yr / 12k | 1 yr / 12k | 1 yr / 12k | 1/12 (Std) or 2/24 (Certified+) | None |
| Hybrid Component coverage | **8 yr / 100k (Hybrid models only)** | n/a (rolled into powertrain) | **10 yr / 150k** (longest hybrid term) | n/a | n/a |
| **EV HV battery coverage** | **10 yr / 100k (EV only)** — same as Hyundai | **10 yr / 100k (EV only)** | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Deductible | **$50 on B2B**, $0 powertrain | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Embedded value (ICE) | $1,200-$2,000 | $1,500-$2,500 | $1,000-$2,000 | $1,200-$1,800 | $2,000-$2,500 |
| Embedded value (Hybrid) | $2,000-$3,000 | (in ICE figure) | $2,500-$4,000 | n/a | n/a |

**Key structural difference**: Kia and Hyundai share the industry-longest 10yr/100k powertrain coverage; Kia has tighter eligibility (5/60k vs 7/80k) but adds explicit 8/100k Hybrid Component coverage that Hyundai does not break out. Kia's $50 B2B deductible is the only material disadvantage vs Hyundai/Toyota/Honda/Subaru ($0). For Kia EV buyers, CPO is structurally hard to beat — same as Hyundai EV CPO logic.
